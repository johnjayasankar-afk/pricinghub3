## Description

Know the real profit on every job — from the first quote to the last payment.

This is a complete job-costing system for small contractors in one workbook: 24 connected sheets that take a job from estimate to proposal, schedule it on a Gantt chart, track crew hours and every cost, handle change orders with a signable form, invoice and collect, and show budget-vs-actual and profit by job on a dashboard. No subscription, no add-ins, no macros.

Built for general contractors, remodelers, handymen, painters, roofers, landscapers, flooring and tile installers, electricians, plumbers and any job-based trade business.

ESTIMATE & SELL
• Estimate: cost code → category → markup applied automatically; override any line
• Cost Library: your own price book — pick an item and the unit and unit cost fill in
• Proposal: client-ready document with scope summary, contingency, tax, payment schedule (deposit / progress / final), up to 40 detailed lines, terms and signature lines. Internal cost and margin stay out of the print area
• Clients directory with lifetime value and balance due

SCHEDULE & TRACK
• Schedule: tasks with workday end dates and a 12-week Gantt chart (blue planned, dark done, yellow today, late turns red)
• Timesheet: crew hours with rates from your crew table, labor burden, labor cost and billable value, plus a weekly payroll summary
• Job Costs: materials, subs, equipment, permits — paid or unpaid
• Mileage log at your rate, counted into job cost
• Daily Log: weather, crew, work performed, issues, inspections — your dispute record
• Change Orders: approved COs raise the contract and the budget automatically; print the CO Form for signature

INVOICE & COLLECT
• Invoices with balance, status (Open / Partial / Paid / Overdue), days overdue and aging bucket
• Invoice Print: printable invoice with contract summary for any invoice number
• Payments linked to invoices and jobs
• Client Statement: every invoice, balance and aging for one client on a printable page — send it monthly
• Invoice Print includes a tear-off remittance slip; CO Form shows the revised completion date; Proposal carries a proposal number plus your exclusions and assumptions

SEE THE NUMBERS
• Dashboard: 16 KPIs (contract value, cost, profit, margin, margin after overhead, invoiced, collected, outstanding, overdue, backlog, unpaid bills, active jobs, over-budget jobs, labor hours this month, win rate), an alerts row, a this-month row, three charts, per-job table, monthly P&L
• Job Profit Report: budget vs actual by category and by cost code with OVER / Watch / On track flags, net margin after overhead, estimated vs actual labor hours, unpaid bills, pending COs, late tasks
• Reports: receivables aging, 6-month cash forecast, pipeline by status, cost-code performance across all jobs
• Tax Summary: year totals by category, quarter-by-quarter table for estimated taxes, mileage, 1099 review list
• Vendors & Crew: insurance-expiry alerts, W-9 and 1099 flags, paid year-to-date and unpaid balance per vendor; weekly overtime flags per worker
• Health Check: counts data problems and formula errors so the reports stay trustworthy
• Polish: suggested next Job ID / invoice # / CO #, dropdown hints, sales tax on materials or all lines, one-click sheet protection (inputs are pre-unlocked)
