# Opportunity scorecard and decision (2026-09-04)

## Feasibility of "paid today"

Not feasible from this session. Every payment mechanism found requires the owner to open or verify a seller account (identity, bank, tax), which I cannot do, and the shortest platform holds are:

| Mechanism | Sale possible | Funds collected | Withdrawable | In bank |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Payhip / Ko-fi / itch.io "direct" | Same day a buyer appears | Instant to PayPal | Instant (PayPal balance) | PayPal to bank 1–3 days | 
| Etsy Payments (new seller) | Days to weeks after listing | At sale | 14 days after sale | Next Monday deposit (weekly default) |
| Apify Store | Weeks | Monthly invoice on 11th | Paid days 21–25 next month, $20 min | Days later |
| Fiverr | Days to weeks | At delivery | 14 days after delivery | PayPal 1–2 days |
| Gumroad | Days to weeks | At sale | Weekly, $100 min until verified | 2–5 days |

The instant channels have no buyer traffic for a new seller. The channel with traffic (Etsy) has a 14-day hold. So the honest target is: first sale within roughly 2–6 weeks of publishing, first bank deposit 2–3 weeks after that.

## Top three examined

1. **Contractor job-costing spreadsheet on Etsy** — passes: verifiable sales in the niche, agent can fulfill fully, automatic delivery, price tier ($19–$29) that survives Etsy fees. Weakness: new-shop visibility and the set-up fee.
2. **Personal budget spreadsheet on Etsy** — rejected: the same effort lands in a $0.99 price war.
3. **Apify utility actor** — kept as fallback: $0, account exists, but demand in every legitimate utility niche is thin and the maintenance duty (respond within 14 days) is real.

Rejected outright for eligibility or rules: Figma (invite-only), Canva Elements (closed), Notion/Framer (cannot build here), Redbubble/Envato/Creative Market (40–50% fees), bounties and Fiverr (not passive; need the owner's identity and third-party contact).

## Decision

- **Primary:** "Contractor Job Costing & Estimate Spreadsheet" sold as an Etsy instant download, with a Payhip storefront as a second, zero-cost checkout for direct links (instant PayPal payout).
- **Fallback:** Apify pay-per-event actor (website audit) if the Etsy listing shows no sale in the evaluation window.

## Stop / pivot conditions

- Evaluation window: 45 days from publication.
- Pivot trigger: fewer than 150 listing views or 0 sales at day 45 despite 2 title/tag revisions.
- Diagnose in order: views (discovery) → favorites/click-through (offer/thumbnail) → sales (price/trust) before changing the product.
