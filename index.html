    Does it work in Google Sheets?

    Yes. Upload the .xlsx to Drive, open it, then File → Save as Google Sheets. Formulas, dropdowns, conditional formatting and the Gantt carry over; the three bar charts may need a one-time reformat.

    Any macros, add-ins or subscription?

    None. Plain formulas you can audit. One-time purchase.

    Can I rename cost codes and add crew?

    Yes. 24 editable codes, 30 crew rows, 100 vendors, and your own price book.

    Can I protect the formulas?

    Yes. Inputs are pre-unlocked, so Review → Protect Sheet locks everything else in one step.

    Is it accounting or tax software?

    No. It is an organizing and estimating tool. The 1099 list and tax summary are informational; confirm with your tax professional.
